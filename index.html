Questions

Good questions.

Do I need any experience?

No. If you can say "hello" in your own language, you are ready. Lessons start from zero.

How long should I practice each day?

Five minutes is plenty. One short lesson a day does more than a long session once a week.

Is it really free?

Yes. The free plan has every language and every lesson. Plus just removes the limits.

Can I learn more than one language?

Of course. Switch any time and your streak follows you.

Who is Remy?

A fox with strong opinions about verbs. He is also very good at cheering.
